# S3 — Data Transformation & Quality — Conversation and Decision Log

This is a durable, concise record of important discussions relevant to this squad. It is not a raw transcript archive. Preserve context needed to understand why work changed direction.

## Logging rules
For each meaningful session record:
- Date and task/workstream.
- Participants/tools (informational only; never imply permanent ownership).
- User request and intended outcome.
- Important conclusions and rationale.
- Options considered and rejected, with reasons.
- Decisions and their status: PROPOSED / UNDER REVIEW / APPROVED / REJECTED / SUPERSEDED.
- Questions, risks, and dependencies.
- Files/commits updated.
- Next action and handoff link.
