# P0 — Core Platform & Developer Experience — Vision

**Status:** PROPOSED — to be validated during workflow research  
**Owner:** Product owner for product direction; squad contributors for discovery and delivery  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-05

## North-star mission
Shared secure foundations and developer experience for all squads.

## Long-term outcome
Provide reusable, reliable platform capabilities. Keep shared primitives generic and leave product-domain semantics with product squads.

## Users and jobs to be understood
Research the primary personas, professional jobs-to-be-done, skill levels, team contexts, scale, privacy constraints, and current tools. Do not assume these are finalized until Stage 4 workflow research.

## Capability horizon
Identity/access integration; project and resource identity; dataset/artifact/job primitives; API/event/schema standards; secrets and security foundations; observability; testing/CI/CD; developer tooling.

## Experience principles
- Professional-grade correctness, transparency, and predictable behavior.
- Clear explanations, useful defaults, and expert-level control when needed.
- Reproducibility, provenance, and traceable outputs.
- Fast feedback and responsive interaction without hiding expensive or asynchronous work.
- Privacy, security, accessibility, and graceful failure.
- Interoperability and escape hatches; avoid unnecessary lock-in.

## Success measures to define
- User task completion and time-to-result.
- Correctness and reproducibility.
- Reliability, performance, and recovery from failure.
- Adoption, repeat usage, and user satisfaction.
- Quality of documentation, tests, and operational readiness.

Numeric targets and baselines: **TBD after research and product-owner approval.**

## Non-goals and boundaries
S1–S7 consume platform contracts. P0 collaborates on cross-cutting contracts and platform operations; it does not own product-specific workflows or domain meaning.
